# Q40 to Q42: Not as Described, Defective and Counterfeit Goods

> **Fictional policy.** Written for the DisputeDesk demo project. These are not real card-network,
> issuer or regulatory rules, and must not be used to decide real disputes.

## When the Q codes apply

The Q codes cover goods that were delivered but are materially different from what the merchant
promised:

- **Q40 Not as described**: wrong item, wrong size or model, used when sold as new, or missing
  features stated in the listing.
- **Q41 Defective or damaged**: arrived broken, or stopped working within the period the merchant
  or listing promised.
- **Q42 Counterfeit**: the item is an imitation of a branded product.

## What does not qualify

- Minor cosmetic differences, such as a slightly different colour shade from the product photo.
- Buyer's remorse or a change of mind.
- Damage caused by the cardholder after delivery.
- Food, drink or other perishables that were consumed.

## Contact and wait requirement

Before filing, the cardholder must contact the merchant to describe the problem and ask for a
fix or refund, and then wait **15 calendar days** from that contact for the merchant to resolve
it. The wait is waived if the merchant refuses in writing during that period.

## Return requirement

For Q40 and Q41, the cardholder must return the goods, or attempt to, and provide return tracking
from a carrier. The return requirement is waived when:

- the merchant refused to accept the return or did not provide a return address,
- return shipping would cost more than **25% of the item price**, or
- the goods are Q42 counterfeit items.

### Counterfeit items are not returned

Counterfeit goods must never be shipped back to the merchant. Instead, the cardholder provides a
written description of how they know the item is counterfeit, and where possible a statement from
the brand owner or an authorised reseller.

## Merchant evidence

The merchant may defeat a Q claim with the product listing as it appeared at the time of sale,
photos of the item taken before shipping, and evidence that a disclosed, reasonable return
policy was offered and the cardholder did not use it.

## Decision rules

- Qualifying difference, contact and return requirements met: **accept**.
- Return requirement not met and no waiver applies: **reject**, and the letter explains that the
  cardholder may refile after returning the item if still within the filing window.
- Merchant shows the item matched the listing: **reject**.
- Partial problem (one item of several): partial refund for the affected items only.
