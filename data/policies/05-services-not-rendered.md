# R21: Services Not Rendered

> **Fictional policy.** Written for the DisputeDesk demo project. These are not real card-network,
> issuer or regulatory rules, and must not be used to decide real disputes.

## When R21 applies

R21 covers payments for something other than physical goods that the merchant did not provide:
travel bookings, hotel stays, events, streaming and software subscriptions, licences and other
digital products. It applies when the service never started, was cancelled by the merchant, or
access was never granted.

## Travel and accommodation

- If the merchant cancelled the flight, tour or hotel stay and did not offer an equivalent
  alternative the cardholder accepted, the dispute is **accepted**.
- If the cardholder cancelled, the merchant's cancellation policy disclosed at booking applies.
  A refund is due only to the extent that policy allows.
- A boarding record, check-in record or hotel folio showing the cardholder used the service is
  compelling evidence and leads to **rejection**.

For bookings with a future service date, the filing window is counted from the scheduled service
date, not the settlement date (see `08-time-limits.md`).

## Subscriptions and recurring billing

A renewal charge is refunded when the cardholder:

1. cancelled before the renewal date using a method the merchant disclosed, and
2. can show a cancellation confirmation number, email or screenshot.

A free trial that converted to a paid plan is **accepted** if the merchant did not clearly
disclose the price and conversion date when the trial started. If the merchant can show that
disclosure and the cardholder did not cancel in time, the dispute is **rejected**.

## Digital products and software

The merchant may defeat an R21 claim for software or streaming access with usage logs showing
that the account was logged in to, or the licence was activated, **after** the purchase date
from a device or location previously used by the cardholder. Logins before the purchase date do
not count.

## Partial service

When only part of a service was provided (for example, two nights of a four-night stay, or a
subscription that stopped working half way through the billing period), the refund is prorated
to the unused portion.

## Not covered by R21

- Dissatisfaction with a meal, show or service that was delivered and consumed.
- Services the cardholder chose not to use when they were available.
